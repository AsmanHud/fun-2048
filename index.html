Pocket Toyworks

Privacy Policy

Effective September 5, 2026. Applies to version 0.1.0.

The game works offline

Pocket Toyworks is a single-player workshop game published by Mulyony Labs. The commissions, customer messages, and delivery terminal are fictional game content. They do not connect to an online service.

Information in the app

This version does not collect or transmit personal information to Mulyony Labs. It contains no accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking SDKs, or in-app purchases. It does not request access to your contacts, location, microphone, or camera.

Local saves and files

Your game progress and settings are stored on your device. The app retains a local backup of a previous valid save to help recover from interrupted or damaged saves. You can export your progress using the operating system's sharing interface and restore a save file you select. Where you send an exported file is your choice; the destination service's policies apply.

Mulyony Labs does not operate a cloud save service. Automatic Android cloud backup is disabled for the app. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app may delete local progress. Keep an exported save if you want to preserve it separately.

Third-party services

The game is distributed through Google Play, which processes information under Google's own policies. This policy page is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information, including IP addresses, when you visit this website under GitHub's privacy statement. Visiting this page is separate from playing the offline game.

Support and children

If you email us, we receive the information you include and use it to respond to your request. Avoid sending sensitive personal information. The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other players.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy if the game's data practices change. Questions or requests about information you have provided through support can be sent to mulyonylabs@gmail.com.
